BellForge Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Quant Design LLC

BellForge ("the app") is made by Quant Design LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: during this beta, your information stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it can work:

  • Workouts you complete, your streak and weekly goal
  • Saved workouts, program progress and contests you create
  • Your level and equipment settings
  • Meal plan inputs you enter, such as age, height, weight, activity level, goal, diet and allergies

This information is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share personal information through the app. BellForge has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data from other apps.

Friends, challenges and meal orders

In this beta, friends, leaderboards and meal delivery orders are sample data inside the app. Invite links and meal orders are not sent anywhere.

Links to other sites

The gear shop opens links to third-party stores, such as Amazon, in your phone's browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Beta testing services

If you install a test version through Google Play testing, Apple TestFlight or Firebase App Distribution, that service may collect information such as your email address, device model and app version to deliver the build and its feedback tools. This is handled under Google's or Apple's privacy policies.

Deleting your information

You can delete everything the app has stored by uninstalling it, or by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings.

Children

BellForge is intended for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Health information

The app's calorie and protein targets are estimates for general fitness and are not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition program.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before adding features that collect information, such as accounts, cloud sync, notifications or real meal orders. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
