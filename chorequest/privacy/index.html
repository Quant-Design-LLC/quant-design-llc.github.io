ChoreQuest Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2025 · Quant Design LLC

ChoreQuest ("the app") is made by Quant Design LLC ("we", "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of children who use the app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

1. Information we collect

Information provided by parents

  • Parent email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Child's first name or nickname (chosen by parent)
  • Child's birth date (to provide age-appropriate content)
  • Household information (to connect family members)

Information collected automatically

  • App usage data (chores completed, rewards earned)
  • Device identifiers for app functionality
  • Crash logs and performance data

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the ChoreQuest experience
  • Track chore completion and reward progress
  • Display age-appropriate quizzes and educational content
  • Enable family collaboration features
  • Send important account notifications to parents

3. Advertising

ChoreQuest may display rewarded video advertisements through Google AdMob. These ads are:

  • Optional — Children choose whether to watch ads to earn bonus coins.
  • Age-appropriate — We use Google's child-directed ad settings.
  • Privacy-focused — We enable child-directed treatment (TFCD) flags.
  • Limited tracking — Personalized advertising is disabled for child users.

We do not serve behavioral or interest-based advertising to children.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share children's personal information with third parties except:

  • With service providers who help operate the app (hosting, analytics)
  • When required by law or to protect safety
  • With parental consent for specific features

5. Parental rights

Parents have the right to:

  • Review their child's personal information
  • Request deletion of their child's data
  • Refuse further collection of their child's information
  • Update or correct their child's information

To exercise these rights, contact us at michael.valente1@gmail.com.

6. Data security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect personal information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication systems
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal data

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services. When an account is deleted, we remove associated personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify parents of material changes via email or in-app notification before they take effect.
